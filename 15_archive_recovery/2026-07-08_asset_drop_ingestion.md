## Cross-plate glyph-name variance (noted, not reconciled)

The three glyph sources disagree on names/rosters and are **not** merged here:
- `NAGO_GLYPHS_SMALL_SYSTEMS_LAB_v1` — 12 glyphs; Care/Nurture = **ÌMÚLÓ**,
  Knowledge/Learning = **AWON-ÒRÒ**, plus ÀLÀÁFÍA (Harmony), ISÉ (Work), ÌFÒWÓPÁMÓ (Community).
- `PLATE_UMADA_TISETAN_TABLET_v1` — 9 glyphs; Care/Nurture = **UMADA**, Knowledge/Order =
  **TISETAN**, plus AYE (adds "Life/Vitality"), IWÒRÍ (Truth), etc.
- The existing realized plates `PLATE_NAGO_SYMBOLOGY_OF_UMADA` / `PLATE_PROTO_NAGO` use the
  survival-sign vocabulary (Bread, Water, Safe, Danger, Carry, Home, Help, Rest) — a
  **different axis** from these value/operation glyphs.
Which roster is canonical, and how the Yorùbá "Nago Glyphs" value-set relates to the locked
"Nago" survival-sign system, is open (see #46).
