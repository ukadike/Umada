# Episode 01 — Cape Wipeout, Pt. 1 — Storyboard v1

Status: **DERIVED PRODUCTION ARTIFACT — first pass.** This storyboard is not new canon.
Like the cold-open previz (`assets/video/EPISODE_01_COLD_OPEN_PREVIZ_v1.mp4`), it is a
panel-by-panel assembly of material already locked elsewhere: the recovered rescue
animatic's beat order (`11_episodes/EPISODE_01_CAPE_WIPEOUT.md`,
`01_canon/VISUAL_CANON_REGISTRY.md`), the draft script
(`11_episodes/EPISODE_01_CAPE_WIPEOUT_SCRIPT.md`), and the five locked concept-art
references. Every caption is verbatim. Every shot description is grounded in an
already-locked fact or reference image; where no visual reference exists, the panel is
marked **[VISUAL DEV NEEDED]** or **[OPEN — #N]** rather than invented — per
`assets/storyboards/README.md` and the no-fabrication rule (`CLAUDE.md`).

**Scope:** matches the animatic's own scope (48.58 s of recovered material) plus the
locked cold-open frame that precedes it. It does not extend the story past the recovered
material (`01_canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` #34).

**Visual layer:** the panels depict the **diegetic world layer** — colorful, weather-rich,
southern-African coastal, never Arctic (`docs/production/VISUAL_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`,
`00_governance/CANON_STATUS.md`). This document itself is presentation-layer text.

**Columns per panel:** Time (animatic timecode where applicable) · Caption (verbatim) ·
Shot & staging (grounded) · Visual source · Notes / open.

## Sequence 2 — EXT. FRIEDMANDOSTORP — AFTERMATH — DAY

Southern Cape corridor, near Cape Agulhas — colorful, weather-rich, painted; never
Arctic.

### Panel 2.1 — The town in shards

- **Time:** 0:05–0:12
- **Caption (verbatim):** "The town is in shards." / "The town is blown open."
- **Shot & staging:** Establishing the blown-open harbor settlement. Colorful diegetic
  world layer, morning coastal light.
- **Visual source:** Animatic 0:05–0:12. Aftermath tone corroborated by
  `assets/concept-art/ADA_WASTELAND_REFERENCE_v1.png` (blast-wreckage aftermath).
- **Notes / open:** Wide establishing; keep painterly harbor palette, not desaturated to
  archive gray.

### Panel 2.2 — The debris detail

- **Time:** 0:12–0:15
- **Caption (verbatim):** "Corrugated roofs. Cement dust. Clay. Sewage. Bread in the road."
- **Shot & staging:** Debris insert. **Bread in the road** is a literal locked detail, not
  set dressing — ties to Friedmandostorp's Agege-bread culture and the Bread Sign's origin
  at this town's bread stop.
- **Visual source:** Animatic 0:12–0:15.
- **Notes / open:** The bread is a plant for the Bread Sign / Nago origin — keep it legible
  in frame.

### Panel 2.3 — A face first

- **Time:** 0:15–0:21
- **Caption (verbatim):** "A face first. Then the damage."
- **Shot & staging:** Push to **Ada's** face. Hair normally slicked into a ponytail,
  mangled by the blast; woodland-camo uniform with name tape, gold collar insignia,
  wings/pilot badge, sleeve flag patch, web belt, sidearm, knife, wristwatch, and the
  silver-chain necklace with a small solid heart pendant (Kassey's gift).
- **Visual source:** `assets/concept-art/ADA_CHARACTER_REFERENCE_v1.png` (wardrobe/gear
  source of truth) + `assets/concept-art/ADA_WASTELAND_REFERENCE_v1.png` (aftermath
  state). Animatic 0:15–0:21.
- **Notes / open:** Ada is the most fully locked character visually — match the reference
  exactly.

### Panel 2.4 — Not dead, not yet

- **Time:** 0:21–0:26
- **Caption (verbatim):** "Not dead. Not yet."
- **Shot & staging:** Ada among the figures in the rubble; a mark resembling **"FFF"** is
  etched near the figures in-frame.
- **Visual source:** Animatic 0:21–0:26.
- **Notes / open:** The "FFF" mark is unexplained and is **not** to be interpreted or
  stylized into meaning (`01_canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` #26) — reproduce as-seen only.

### Panel 2.5 — One name burning

- **Time:** 0:26–0:30
- **Caption (verbatim):** "And one name burning under all of it."
- **Shot & staging:** Insert — a torn military record. Stamped **CLEARANCE DENIED**.
  Legible fragments "…VENTER", "…STED KIA" (likely "LISTED KIA"), descriptor line
  "[C]HIMERA SURVIVOR. SCIENTIST. EMPATH." (Ada's descriptors). Burned across it in a
  different, glowing script: the name **Kassey**.
- **Visual source:** Animatic 0:26–0:30; caption transcript in
  `01_canon/VISUAL_CANON_REGISTRY.md`.
- **Notes / open:** Why Kassey's name burns across Ada's record specifically is shown but
  not explained (`01_canon/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` #30) — do not resolve it visually.

### Panel 2.6 — The broken road

- **Time:** 0:30–0:33
- **Caption (verbatim):** "The road comes broken: paperwork, fire, betrayal — and Kassey."
- **Shot & staging:** Continues the record/aftermath motif. **Kassey is not shown** — no
  visual reference for him exists.
- **Visual source:** Animatic 0:30–0:33. Kassey: [VISUAL DEV NEEDED] / [OPEN — #30].
- **Notes / open:** Do not design Kassey. Whether he and Ada acted willingly or under
  coercion is open (#30).

### Panel 2.7 — The body says no

- **Time:** 0:33–0:37
- **Caption (verbatim):** "The record says dead. The body says no."
- **Shot & staging:** A cloaked figure cradling another body: this is **Tallai** — pale
  scaled reptilian skin, frilled head-crest, clawed/webbed hand, white care-coat with a
  patch reading **C.A.R.F.** (Kemi, 2026-06-24) — tending the wounded Ada, cradling her
  forehead. Tallai's first instinct is not fear but understanding (interior beat, not a
  spoken line).
- **Visual source:** `assets/concept-art/TALLAI_ADA_RESCUE_REFERENCE_v1.png` (locked).
  Animatic 0:33–0:37.
- **Notes / open:** This is the emotional center; the rescue-reference image is the exact
  staging. C.A.R.F. patch is now legible canon — render it legibly.
