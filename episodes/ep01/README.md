# UMADA — Cape Wipeout, Pt. 1: An Omoluabi Witness Record
**Episode 1 Draft 1 · Public Prototype v0.1**

UMADA is the world. Omoluabi is the witnessing system.

**Cape Wipeout, Pt. 1** is the first public interactive witness record from UMADA. Built through the Omoluabi system, the episode asks audiences to investigate a historical catastrophe through bodies, records, witnesses, environmental signals, and an archival device that may itself be damaged.

Two hundred years after the Cape Wipeout, the houses of Umada and Tisetan send a Reporter into the past carrying an Omoluabi device. The audience enters that record and follows evidence across Friedmandorstrop: Tallai's bread route, the laboratory garden, Ada's military feed, and the Reporter's anomaly log.

The record says Ada is dead before the blast occurs. Her body says otherwise.

The audience may choose what to examine and which contradictions to preserve. The audience may not rewrite history.

## Release note
This release demonstrates the connection between UMADA's speculative history and Omoluabi's human-governed editorial intelligence. The experience is intentionally incomplete. Some fragments, doctrine language, accessibility behaviors, visual details, and canon decisions remain in development.
