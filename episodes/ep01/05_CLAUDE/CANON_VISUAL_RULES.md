# Visual Canon Rules — Episode 1

## Tallai

The approved reference is `02_REFERENCES/TALLAI_CANONICAL_VISUAL_REFERENCE.png`.

Tallai is not an ordinary human caregiver and not a generic fantasy warrior.

Required continuity:

- visibly nonhuman chimera anatomy;
- adult and tall;
- long pointed ears;
- scaled / plated cranial structure;
- fine skin patterning;
- pale layered care garments;
- composed, observant, compassionate medical presence;
- consistent face and anatomy in bread-route, blast, rescue, stabilization, and later scenes.

## Ada

- adult Nigerian field commander;
- never portrayed as a child;
- capable and alert before the blast;
- severely injured but alive afterward;
- BODY and RECORD contradiction must remain visually legible.

## Reporter

- face never shown;
- rear silhouette, hood, shadow, gloves, partial framing;
- carries the Omoluabi device;
- witnesses and records rather than rewriting events.

## Q

- small insect-like chimera;
- sensory and detection role;
- should read as intelligent and responsive, not a pet insect.

## Quartz

- massive gentle protector and load-bearer;
- should display tenderness as well as strength;
- finds and preserves the dusty teddy bear;
- avoid reducing Quartz to a hostile monster.

## Visual language

- first four episodes: monochrome / sepia charcoal, etched engraving, weathered archival paper;
- no glossy superhero treatment;
- no anime or conventional comic-book styling;
- captions and interface labels should remain separate from clean story images whenever possible.
