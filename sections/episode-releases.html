Episode Releases

Three episode releases are named in the build spec. Cape Wipeout, Pt. 1 has a public Draft 1 prototype below; the other two scaffold the release structure so each can be generated precisely from canon once it's ready.

  • UMADA — Cape Wipeout, Pt. 1: An Omoluabi Witness Record

    EPISODE 1 DRAFT 1 · PUBLIC PROTOTYPE v0.1

    Open the interactive witness record

    Cape Wipeout, Pt. 1 is the first public interactive witness record from UMADA. Built through the Omoluabi system, the episode asks audiences to investigate a historical catastrophe through bodies, records, witnesses, environmental signals, and an archival device that may itself be damaged.

    This is a public working prototype, not the final canonical Episode 1. Canon, accessibility, visual details, and story fragments remain under active review.

    Related canon: Era 5 — Cape Wipeout; Ada, Tallai/Talla, Q, and Quartz; the Rescue Doctrine.

    Visual reference: the Friedmandostorp rescue animatic.
