Episode One · Story Guide · Current canon

Cape Wipeout, Part One

A civilization arguing about its future is forced to reopen the evidence of its beginning.

In the Council Era, about two centuries after Cape Wipeout, Tisetan’s Council asks Ada Venter to return to the event that shaped its history. Ada refuses to let her survival become public property. She chooses a faceless, shrouded Reporter to carry the witness device Lùabi back into the record.

Follow the record

  1. The Council’s question. Tisetan’s leaders revisit Cape Wipeout as they debate the future of the capital and its Houses.
  2. Ada sends a witness. The Reporter is sent to observe the past, not change it. Their identity remains undisclosed.
  3. A lived home. The record returns to Friedmandorstorp on March 19, 2226, before the catastrophe: a market, bread, workers, children, and research woven into everyday life.
  4. The record arrives first. Five warnings converge. At 08:58, the official system marks Ada as killed in action before the 09:00 strike. Ada’s body is still alive.
  5. Care becomes evidence. The strike is ordered to erase the laboratory and suppress evidence of the concealed outbreak. After the blast, Q detects life, Tallai finds Ada’s pulse and orders extraction, and Quartz carries her to safety.

KC is expected at 09:00. The episode leaves KC’s fate unresolved. The rescue—not the destruction—is the emotional center of this record.

Two names, two contexts

Lùabi is the fictional recording device carried by the Reporter. Omoluabi is the real-world project connected to the work; it is not the device’s in-world name.
