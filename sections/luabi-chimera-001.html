The Baboons Knew First

Luabi detects that apparently separate ecological incidents form a southward pattern converging on Cape Agulhas. It recognizes the relationship before it knows the cause.

LUABI-CHIMERA-001 LOCKED

LUABI: These are not separate incidents. Follow the animals south.

Correlation before explanation

Luabi does not begin by identifying chimera flu. It compares weak signals that humans have divided among wildlife management, veterinary medicine, ecology, weather, ocean systems, biological sampling, animal movement, and MIC activity.

The pattern moves from baboon anomalies into cross-species signals and then toward Cape Agulhas.

The chain

baboon anomalies → wider animal signals → Cape Agulhas → hidden MIC chimera research / containment breach → chimera-flu leakage → Cape Wipeout → Hurricane Cindy dispersal

Sentinel animals

The baboons function as sentinel animals in the narrative. Human institutions treat them primarily as a management problem while Luabi recognizes that their behavior forms part of a larger ecological warning.

The animals were treated as the problem while they were transmitting evidence.

What Luabi actually knows

At the first alert, Luabi does not know that a chimera pathogen is responsible. It knows that the incidents are unlikely to be independent and that the geographic and biological correlations justify following the signal south.

This preserves a core Luabi doctrine: the system may identify a relationship without pretending to possess an explanation that the evidence has not yet established.

Cape Agulhas and MIC

Later evidence connects the convergence point to hidden MIC chimera research associated with a coastal / undersea facility near Cape Agulhas. The containment breach becomes part of the causal sequence leading to Cape Wipeout, destruction and suppression of evidence, and later dispersal through Hurricane Cindy.

Reality / fiction boundary

South African baboon-human conflict is real-world research stimulus. The chimera-flu connection, MIC facility, Luabi inference, Cape Wipeout, and Hurricane Cindy chain are UMADA speculative fiction and are not claims about real animals, institutions, outbreaks, or scientific findings.
