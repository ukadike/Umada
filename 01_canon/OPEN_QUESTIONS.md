# Open Questions

A living register. Nothing below is invented — each item is either explicitly flagged as
open in a source document, or is a real gap noticed while reconciling the source package
against the realized plates. See `00_governance/TINY_QUESTIONS_PROTOCOL.md` for how the
smallest of these get asked to Kemi one at a time.

## Naming & canon-structure decisions (Kemi's to confirm or change)

1. **Memory vs Witness.** Applied as a rename ("Witness" → "Memory" family) per the
   refined plate. If Witness and Memory were meant as two distinct families, say so and
   they'll be split.
2. **Environment family contents.** Seeded as EMERGING with no signs yet — no canon
   existed to seed from.
3. **Motif-five fate.** The five motif families (Wound, Relay, Archive, Storm, Visitor)
   are kept as a live narrative axis, separate from the seven signage families. Could be
   retired in favor of the plate's Visual Grammar primitives (Circle, Open Circle,
   Broken Circle, Three Rings, Hand+Ring) if Kemi prefers.
4. **Talla vs Tallai — RESOLVED (Kemi, 2026-06-23): converge on Tallai.** Both forms were
   attested; Kemi confirmed a single converged spelling. **"Talla"** is RETIRED as a name
   form — see `00_governance/CANON_STATUS.md`. Verbatim quotes of the rescue animatic's
   caption ("Talla calls...") remain unchanged as literal transcript, not as ongoing use
   of the retired form.

## Nago / signage

5. **Proto-Nago marks that didn't carry forward.** `PLATE_PROTO_NAGO` documents fourteen
   marks (including Go This Way, Care/Love, Look/Watch, Gather, Down/Below, Up/Above,
   Stop/No); the refined plate's core set is eight (seven of the fourteen carried
   forward, plus Rest, which has no proto-layer precursor). Were the seven that didn't
   carry forward dropped, absorbed into the seven function families some other way, or
   simply not yet re-rendered in the refined system?
6. **Visual Grammar primitives as buildable shapes.** The plate shows Circle, Open
   Circle, Broken Circle, Three Rings, and Hand+Ring constructed against the Golden
   Rectangle, but no vector/CSS-level shape spec exists yet for the site to render them
   precisely rather than approximate them.
7. **Quartz absent from the plate's Honor & Origin line.** The refined plate credits
   Nago as "carried by Tallai, Ada, Q, and the people," but Quartz is locked elsewhere
   as a fourth Cape Wipeout survivor and load-bearer/rescue anchor. Intentional omission,
   or an artifact of the plate predating Quartz's full lock-in?
8. **What mark would Tallai recognize first?** (Symbology Bible tiny question, not
   resolved by either plate.)

## Black Stars / Earth Sensors Lab expansion (Kemi, 2026-06-25)

36. **Is Earth Sensors Lab the same referent as the SSL Layer / Small Systems Lab? —
    RESOLVED (Kemi, 2026-06-25): they are different.** `01_canon/FOUNDATIONAL_STRATA.md`
    item 9 names an undeveloped "SSL Layer — UMADA as mythological/narrative layer of
    Small Systems Lab"; the Black Stars brief names "Earth Sensors Lab" as a present-day
    educational program and the "educational ancestor of Black Star science." Kemi
    confirmed these are **two genuinely separate concepts**, not one under two names —
    they are not merged. See `01_canon/BLACK_STARS_CIVILIZATION.md` and
    `01_canon/FOUNDATIONAL_STRATA.md`.
37. **Where does the Kuiper Belt Renaissance sit on the Era 0–16 timeline?** The Black
    Stars are tied to Era 13 (The Tower) and Era 15 (The Kingdom Age) via the existing
    Tower fact, but the brief does not place the Kuiper Belt Renaissance — the
    deep-space exploration era — at a specific point relative to those eras or any
    other. Not placed by inference.
38. **Scope of invention for the Black Stars' institutions, laboratories, vessels, and
    terminology.** The brief's "Guidance for Claude Code" section explicitly asks for
    new scientific institutions, laboratories, exploration vessels, terminology,
    observational ethics, and additional sensor classes to be invented, which is in
    tension with the no-fabrication rule. **Partially directed (Kemi, 2026-06-25): fill
    one narrower piece — a single exploration vessel — and leave the rest open.**
    Aderin proposed *First Light* and **Kemi confirmed it (2026-06-25)**; it is now the
    first named Black Star vessel at EMERGING status
    (`01_canon/BLACK_STARS_CIVILIZATION.md`, `data/black_star_vessels.json`).
    Institutions, laboratories, an observational-ethics doctrine, additional sensor
    classes, and any further vessels remain open, not invented. See
    `13_fragments/2026-06-25_black_stars_canonical_expansion.md`.
