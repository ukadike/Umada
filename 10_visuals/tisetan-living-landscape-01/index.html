Text description of this landscape

Tisetan appears as a layered monochrome field: a low horizon, tower-like silhouettes, broken lines of civic infrastructure, drifting particulate traces, and three bright signal points. The scene changes slowly unless reduced motion is enabled. Every interactive encounter is also available as a standard HTML control above, so the canvas is never required to understand or operate the experience.
