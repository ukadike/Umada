# Angel Dust — Creative Direction Note

**Status:** creator direction captured; canon details require reconciliation with existing Angel Dust records before promotion  
**Captured:** 2026-08-22

## Core image

Angel Dust should be understood visually and atmospherically as **nuclear winter**.

This is the governing environmental feeling to preserve as the visual work develops: a world under an obscuring, particulate sky; diminished or altered daylight; atmospheric density; cold, suspended aftermath; the sense that the environment itself has become a persistent condition rather than a passing weather event.

## Important distinction

“Nuclear winter” is the **visual / experiential analogy**, not an authorization to add a nuclear detonation to UMADA history. Do not infer an atomic cause unless separately established in canon.

Angel Dust remains tied to the established Angel Dust event/virus material. This note records how the creator remembers the resulting world-state and how future landscape studies should feel.

## Landscape study direction

A future p5 landscape can explore:

- particulate fall / suspended dust;
- obscured horizon and weak daylight;
- reduced visibility and flattened distance;
- slow atmospheric accumulation;
- silhouettes emerging and disappearing through the field;
- environmental state communicated through motion, density, sound and interface rather than explanatory text alone;
- a reduced-motion mode that preserves the oppressive atmospheric condition without continuous particle animation.

## Governance

Before Landscape 03 is labeled canonical, reconcile this direction against the current timeline, civilizational ledger, Angel Dust references, and visual canon. Preserve ambiguity where the source material does not establish mechanism, duration, chemistry, or exact appearance.
