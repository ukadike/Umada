A Florida beach under a pale afternoon sky. Children play near the water. Older adults sit beneath a shade structure. Nothing is yet identified as danger.

Canon boundary

Creator memory: Angel Dust should feel like nuclear winter. This visualization uses dimmed sunlight, particulate haze, loss of horizon clarity, ecological stress, and shelter behavior as visual language. It does not establish radiation, nuclear weapons, nuclear causation, exact weather values, or a specific Florida city.
