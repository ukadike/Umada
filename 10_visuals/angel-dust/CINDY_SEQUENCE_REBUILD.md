# Hurricane Cindy / Angel Dust — p5 Sequence Rebuild

**Status:** active creative direction / research-informed speculative visualization  
**Canon boundary:** research informs plausibility and dramatic systems behavior; it does not automatically become UMADA canon.

## Research background

Annie Jacobsen's 2026 discussion of *Biological War: A Scenario* is useful background for imagining the tempo, uncertainty, mobility, institutional stress, and social consequences of a severe biological catastrophe. The UMADA sequence should borrow systems logic rather than copy Jacobsen's fictional pathogen or scenario.

Research reference:
- Jordan Harbinger Show, “Annie Jacobsen | The Genes and Germs of Biological Warfare,” Aug. 10, 2026: https://www.jordanharbinger.com/annie-jacobsen-the-genes-and-germs-of-biological-warfare/

Relevant research ideas for speculative worldbuilding:
- biological catastrophe can begin without an immediately legible visual signature;
- early symptoms and signals may be ambiguous;
- time-to-recognition matters;
- ordinary human mobility can accelerate geographic spread;
- specialized medical/isolation capacity can be overwhelmed quickly;
- multiple agencies may possess partial, contradictory signals;
- catastrophe attacks trust, communication, logistics, medicine, and social systems as well as bodies.

## Creative correction

Retire the current “giant fantasy dust wall approaches the beach” interpretation.

Cindy should initially read as a recognizable hurricane. The audience and characters believe they understand the threat. The biological dimension is detected indirectly and understood later.

The visual drama is therefore **knowledge arriving too late**, not a monster cloud.

## New p5 visual grammar

p5 is not responsible for drawing crude people or scenery. The final production should use canonical black-and-white charcoal/ink landscape plates and storyboard art as image layers. p5 supplies time, parallax, weather, signal overlays, changing visibility, particulate accumulation, camera movement, data transitions, and interaction.

Until final plates are ingested, the p5 prototype should prioritize systems visualization over placeholder stick figures.

## Sequence

### 01 — WATCH
Florida is alive before landfall. Weather services track Hurricane Cindy. Evacuation zones, radar, shelters, roads, hospitals, airports, coastal cameras, environmental sensors, and ordinary movement coexist on screen.

### 02 — PREPARE
The hurricane warning becomes dominant. Evacuation traffic increases. Shelters open. Older adults and children are moved. Hospitals prepare for storm injuries. The interface still categorizes the event as weather.

### 03 — ANOMALY
Before or around landfall, small signals cease agreeing with the hurricane model. Environmental observations, public-health reports, sensor anomalies, and operational messages appear as incomplete fragments. No single panel announces “biological event.” The viewer must notice contradiction.

### 04 — LANDFALL
Cindy crosses the coast. Weather data becomes noisy and infrastructure degrades. The biological event remains largely invisible at human scale. Communications and sensing become less reliable exactly when interpretation matters most.

### 05 — FALSE AFTERMATH
The storm passes. People emerge and cleanup begins. Roads, airports, buses, shelters, family reunification, aid distribution, and return travel create a mobility visualization. The audience now understands that recovery behavior may also be carrying the catastrophe outward.

### 06 — SIGNAL
Hospital/intake markers begin appearing in multiple locations. The pattern does not map neatly to storm injury. Public-health signals, calls, admissions, and geographic clusters begin to align. The system changes classification from WEATHER EVENT to UNKNOWN COMPOUND EVENT.

### 07 — RECOGNITION
The interface shows competing interpretations and confidence levels. Medical capacity tightens. Mobility continues. The biological dimension is recognized after geographic spread is already underway.

### 08 — ANGEL DUST
Only now does the sequence transition toward the established nuclear-winter-like Angel Dust atmosphere: damaged light, dimmed skies, particulate accumulation, ecological stress, reduced visibility, shelter and survival. Do not imply nuclear causation or radiation.

## Human scale

Children, elders, disabled people, caregivers, responders, hospital workers, evacuees, and families remain present, but through authored storyboard plates rather than procedural stick figures. Human vulnerability and interdependence are part of the event, not decoration around a data visualization.

## Interaction

The viewer can scrub through time or allow the sequence to play. Selecting a signal opens a ledger-style evidence card identifying:
- what is observed;
- which system observed it;
- when it was observed;
- confidence / uncertainty;
- what decision-makers currently believe;
- what the audience may infer only in retrospect.

Reduced-motion mode replaces animated flows and weather with discrete state changes. Every visual state receives a textual equivalent.

## Core dramatic principle

**The hurricane is legible. The biological catastrophe is not.**

The viewer watches institutions and communities respond rationally to the event they can see while another event propagates through the same systems of evacuation, shelter, care, travel, cleanup, and reunion.
