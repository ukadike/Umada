# Angel Dust — Atmosphere / Visual Memory Note

**Status:** creator memory captured for canon review  
**Do not silently promote this note to LOCKED canon.**

## Creator recollection

Angel Dust should carry the environmental feeling of a **nuclear winter**.

This is not a claim, at this stage, that Angel Dust *is* nuclear fallout or that a nuclear event caused it. The useful visual/worldbuilding distinction is:

- the **virus/event** and the **environmental aftermath** are separate questions;
- the aftermath can evoke nuclear winter: obscured light, atmospheric particulate, cold/dim skies, reduced visibility, ash/dust-like accumulation, ecological stress, and a world in which daylight itself feels damaged;
- do not add radiation, nuclear weapons, nuclear causation, exact temperatures, duration, chemistry, or epidemiological mechanics unless those are recovered/approved elsewhere in canon.

## Production implication

A future Angel Dust landscape study should feel materially different from Dead Glass. Dead Glass concerns failed technological/information infrastructure; Angel Dust should be read first through atmosphere, ecology, visibility, bodies, shelter, and survival under a dimmed sky.

This note exists so that the phrase **“Angel Dust was like nuclear winter”** is not lost while the visual and narrative canon is being consolidated.
