Text description of this animated field

The screen is monochrome. Thin routes connect block-like civic structures, then break, fade, or terminate before reaching their destinations. Small packets of light attempt to travel along the routes. Some stop abruptly; some fragment into noise; some never leave their source. Reduced-motion mode freezes the movement while preserving all three inspectable states in text and controls.
